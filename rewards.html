Good to know

Questions, answered.

What happens after I submit a photo?+

We check that the photo is yours, the NoodleBomb bottle or jar is visible, and any public post includes the reward disclosure. If approved, we'll email instructions to claim one free eligible Pour or Shake product.

Does the Photo Reward cover shipping?+

The reward covers one eligible product single. Shipping and tax are separate. Automatic free U.S. shipping still starts at $29.99.

Do I have to say something positive?+

No. We want your real photo and how you actually used NoodleBomb—not a scripted or positive review. Public posts just need to clearly disclose that a free product was promised.

How do I earn Bomb Points?+

You earn 5 Bomb Points for every $1 on every order. You also get 250 Bomb Points for joining, 100 for sharing on Facebook, and 250 on your birthday.

What are Bomb Points worth?+

100 points = $1 off. Redeem from 100 points up - so 500 points is $5 off, 1,000 is $10, and so on.

How do I redeem?+

Open the Rewards panel (the tab at the corner of any page, or the button below), choose how many Bomb Points to spend, and you'll get a discount code. Apply it at checkout - that simple.

Do my points expire?+

No. Your Bomb Points stay in your account until you're ready to use them.

Is it free, and how do I join?+

Completely free. Hit Join the Flavor Club, sign up with your email, and your 250 welcome Bomb Points are waiting. Earn on every order you place with that email.
